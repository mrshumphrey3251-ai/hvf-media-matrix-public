**Humphrey Virtual Farm Declassifies Project Ebony: The End of Cloud-Dependent Autonomous Machinery**

**[CITY, STATE] — August 2026** — Humphrey Virtual Farm (HVF) today announced the declassification of Project Ebony, an open-core, vendor-neutral architectural specification designed to eradicate the agricultural and defense sectors' dangerous reliance on cloud connectivity and probabilistic software.

Currently, autonomous machinery relies on internet connectivity to maintain safety floors. Project Ebony completely severs this dependency, utilizing sub-10ms bare-metal hardware interlocks and cryptographic attestation to enforce a deterministic physical safety perimeter. 

"If your machinery requires an internet connection to guarantee it won't execute a catastrophic kinetic fault, you do not have a safety floor. You have a vulnerability," stated Jeffery Humphrey, CEO and Lead Architect at HVF.

The complete architectural blueprints are now available for public audit and OEM integration.
