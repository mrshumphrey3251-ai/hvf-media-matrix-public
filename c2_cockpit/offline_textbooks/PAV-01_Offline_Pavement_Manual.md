## Module-by-Module Reading & Exam Focus Directives (Units 01 - 10)
- **Unit 01 — Assigned Reading:** `23 CFR Part 515 (ODOT TAMP) & ASTM D6433 Highway Asset Governance Manual Ch. 1`  
  *What to Focus On for the Exam Gate:* Focus on National Highway System (NHS) performance metrics, Oklahoma Title 61 corridor auditing, and sample unit sizing (2,500 +/- 1,000 sq ft asphalt).
- **Unit 02 — Assigned Reading:** `ASTM D6433 Standard Practice for Roads and Parking Lots Pavement Condition Index (PCI)`  
  *What to Focus On for the Exam Gate:* Focus on distress density percentage, Deduct Value (DV) curves, Corrected Deduct Value (CDV), and computing PCI = 100 - max(CDV) across 0-100 rating tiers.
- **Unit 03 — Assigned Reading:** `ASTM E950 / AASHTO R 56 Longitudinal Profilometry & International Roughness Index (IRI)`  
  *What to Focus On for the Exam Gate:* Focus on quarter-car golden-car simulation at 50 mph (80 km/h), calculating IRI (in/mile), and federal FHWA good/fair/poor smoothness thresholds (< 95 / > 170 in/mi).
- **Unit 04 — Assigned Reading:** `AASHTO R 87 Transverse Rutting Profilometry & Hydroplaning Physics Manual`  
  *What to Focus On for the Exam Gate:* Focus on 5-point/laser transverse rut depth measurement (inches), Horne hydroplaning speed V_p = 10.35 * sqrt(P_tire), and ponding water film thickness.
- **Unit 05 — Assigned Reading:** `ASTM D4694 / D4695 Falling Weight Deflectometer (FWD) & Backcalculation Manual`  
  *What to Focus On for the Exam Gate:* Focus on impulse load plate pressure, deflection basin sensors (D_0 through D_60), and backcalculating subgrade resilient modulus M_R (psi).
- **Unit 06 — Assigned Reading:** `AASHTO 1993 Guide for Design of Pavement Structures (ESALs & Structural Number)`  
  *What to Focus On for the Exam Gate:* Focus on the 4th-Power Load Equivalency Factor LEF = (W_axle / 18,000)^4, cumulative 18-kip ESALs, and Structural Number SN = a_1*D_1 + a_2*D_2*m_2 + a_3*D_3*m_3.
- **Unit 07 — Assigned Reading:** `FHWA HDS-5 Hydraulic Design of Highway Culverts & Right-of-Way Drainage Manual`  
  *What to Focus On for the Exam Gate:* Focus on Manning open-channel flow Q = (1.486/n) * A * R_h^(2/3) * S^(1/2), inlet vs. outlet control headwater depth (HW/D), and LiDAR silt-blockage profiling.
- **Unit 08 — Assigned Reading:** `FHWA MUTCD (23 CFR Part 655) Retroreflectivity & Guardrail Safety Audit Manual`  
  *What to Focus On for the Exam Gate:* Focus on ASTM E1710 retroreflectometer geometry (30-meter), minimum mcd/m^2/lx pavement marking thresholds, and MASH W-beam guardrail splice height (31.0 in).
- **Unit 09 — Assigned Reading:** `U.S. Access Board PROWAG / ADA Title II Pedestrian Right-of-Way Compliance Manual`  
  *What to Focus On for the Exam Gate:* Focus on curb-ramp running slope (<= 8.33% / 1:12), cross slope (<= 2.00% / 1:48), and smart-level / LiDAR digital inclinometer verification.
- **Unit 10 — Assigned Reading:** `ODOT 8-Year Construction Work Plan (CWP) & Title 61 WORM Corridor Ledger Spec`  
  *What to Focus On for the Exam Gate:* Focus on remaining service life (RSL), preservation vs. reconstruction trigger gates, and sealing corridor GIS/PCI telemetry into matrix_ledger.db.
