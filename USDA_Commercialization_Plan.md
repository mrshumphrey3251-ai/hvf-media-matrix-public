# USDA SBIR PHASE I: COMMERCIALIZATION PLAN
## APPLICANT: Humphrey Virtual Farm (CAGE: 1aha8)

### 1. Market Need & Customer Profile
Small and mid-sized agricultural operations require real-time data processing for autonomous machinery, irrigation control, and crop yield optimization. However, rural broadband dead zones make cloud-reliant AI economically unviable and operationally impossible. Project Ebony provides an air-gapped, sovereign edge-computing matrix that operates entirely off-grid.

### 2. Revenue Model & Go-To-Market Strategy
Humphrey Virtual Farm will monetize via Sovereign Deployment Licenses (SDL). Agricultural enterprises purchase the localized heavy-iron matrix architecture for direct deployment on farm infrastructure. This eliminates ongoing cloud subscription fees, protects proprietary farm yield data from third-party harvesting, and guarantees 100% uptime regardless of rural connectivity.

### 3. Executive Leadership & Capacity
The enterprise is led by Jeffery Humphrey, CEO and Chief Architect, commanding sole proprietary control over the Project Ebony architecture and operational execution from Humphrey Virtual Farm's heavy-iron infrastructure.
