# TREASURY MATRIX: STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (PUBLIC BLUEPRINT)
**Effective Date:** September 2, 2026
**Status:** Active

## 1. BANKING INFRASTRUCTURE 
The Joint Venture operates on a rigid, zero-leakage treasury matrix. All capital flows through a centralized Master Receiving Account under the exclusive custodianship of the primary executive.

## 2. ALLOCATION MECHANICS
Upon clearance of gross revenue, funds are immediately swept into strictly siloed sub-accounts:
*   **Tax Escrow:** Rigidly protected for federal and state liabilities.
*   **Operating Expenses (OpEx):** Deployed strictly under Dual-Signature Authorization.
*   **Capital Expenditures (CapEx):** Locked for joint R&D and technological scaling.
*   **Founder Distributions:** Yield pool, executed within an unyielding Net-15 SLA.
