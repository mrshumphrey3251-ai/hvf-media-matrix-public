# FMS Interoperability & Hardware Integration
**Redacted Public Overview**

Humphrey Virtual Farm engineers Ebony to act as the central nervous system of your operation, featuring native interoperability with legacy and modern agricultural systems.

## Technical Capabilities
* **FMS Connectors:** Pre-built, OAuth 2.0-secured integrations with the top 5 Farm Management Systems (Climate FieldView, Trimble, Granular, etc.).

* **Hardware Agnostic:** Standardized endpoints guarantee that any edge device, from legacy tractors to next-generation drone swarms, interfaces natively with our core.
