## 1. PURPOSE & STATUTORY MANDATE
Federal regulations mandate that all applicants to the Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) and Small Business Technology Transfer (STTR) programs register with the Small Business Administration (SBA) Company Registry at SBIR.gov prior to proposal submission. 

The registration assigns a unique **Small Business Concern Identification (SBC ID)**, which must be entered into **Volume 1 (Proposal Cover Sheet)**.

---

## 2. STEP-BY-STEP REGISTRATION PROCEDURE

### Step 1: Navigate to the SBA Company Registry
1. Open a secure web browser and navigate directly to: `https://www.sbir.gov/registration`
2. Click **"Log In / Register"** and select **"Login.gov"** to authenticate using your executive administrative credentials.

### Step 2: Create / Link Company Profile
1. Enter your corporate Unique Entity Identifier: `S1M4ENLHTDH5`
2. Enter your corporate Commercial and Government Entity Code: `1AHA8`
3. Enter your primary corporate notification address: `humphreyvirtualfarm@gmail.com`
4. Confirm legal entity name matches exactly: **Humphrey Virtual Farms LLC**

### Step 3: Complete Ownership & Certification Disclosures
1. **Ownership Type:** Select Limited Liability Company (LLC).
2. **Citizenship:** Confirm 100% United States citizen ownership.
3. **Employee Count:** Enter current employee count (including Managing Member).
4. **Foreign Affiliations:** Confirm 0% Foreign Ownership, Control, or Influence (FOCI) in alignment with Volume 6 disclosures.

### Step 4: Issue and Record SBC ID
1. Submit the registration form. The SBA system immediately issues an alphanumeric **SBC ID** (e.g., `SBC_123456789`).
2. Download the official **SBA Confirmation PDF** for federal proposal audit records.
3. Record the generated SBC ID in `docs/compliance/FEDERAL_PROPOSAL_REGISTRATION_LEDGER.md`.
