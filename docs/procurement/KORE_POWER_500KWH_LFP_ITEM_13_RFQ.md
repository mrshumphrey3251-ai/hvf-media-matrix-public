# HVF Procurement: KORE Power 500 kWh LFP Storage (Item 13) RFQ
**Issuing Entity:** Humphrey Virtual Farms LLC (CAGE: 1AHA8 | UEI: S1M4ENLHTDH5)  
**Budget Allocation:** $175,000.00 (CapEx, Contractor-Acquired Property)  
**Target Vendors:** KORE Power / American Battery Factory / Blue Energy  

## Technical Requirements
- **Chemistry & Capacity:** Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4 / LFP), 500 kWh nameplate capacity (minimum 475 kWh usable at 80% DoD).
- **Nominal Bus Voltage:** 600V–750V DC nominal operating window; compatible with Victron 480V inverter stack and 150kW turbine rectifier.
- **Continuous & Peak Discharge:** Continuous discharge rate >= 150 kW (0.3C); peak surge discharge capability >= 300 kW for >= 15 seconds to support concrete mixer motor-starts.
- **NDAA & Defense Compliance:** Full compliance with NDAA Section 889, NDAA Section 855, and DFARS 225.7018 (prohibition on covered foreign battery chemistries from PRC/DPRK/Russia/Iran). Certificate of Origin required.
- **Thermal Management & Safety:** Integrated liquid glycol thermal cooling/heating loop; UL 9540A unit-level fire safety and thermal runaway containment certification.
- **BMS & Telemetry:** Hardened Battery Management System (BMS) with isolated CAN 2.0B and Modbus TCP outputs delivering real-time cell-level voltage, temperature, SoC, SoH, and isolation resistance metrics.
- **Packaging:** Low-profile subframe-mount or slide-out tray architecture engineered for 53-ft drop-deck trailer low center of gravity.
