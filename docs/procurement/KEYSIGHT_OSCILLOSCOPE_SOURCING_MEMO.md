## 1. TECHNICAL JUSTIFICATION FOR PHASE I HIL TESTING
To satisfy the Department of Defense (DoD) SBIR Phase I feasibility requirements, Humphrey Virtual Farms LLC must mathematically and visually prove that the Solid-State Silicon Carbide (SiC) MOSFET protection circuit successfully quenched a 480-Volt arc-fault in less than 40 nanoseconds.

Validating sub-40ns latency requires instrumentation capable of sampling at a minimum of 5 Giga-Samples per second (GSa/s) with at least 1 GHz of analog bandwidth to prevent waveform distortion during extreme voltage transients.

## 2. PROCUREMENT SPECIFICATIONS
* **Target Instrument:** Keysight InfiniiVision 3000G X-Series (Model: DSOX3104G).
* **Analog Bandwidth:** 1 GHz (4 Analog Channels).
* **Sampling Rate:** 5 GSa/s half-channel, 2.5 GSa/s all-channel.
* **Required Probes:** High-Voltage Differential Probes (rated for 1000V DC-link measurement) and Rogowski Coil Current Probes (rated for 500A peak transient current).
