## 1. TECHNICAL JUSTIFICATION FOR PHASE I HIL TESTING
The core technical objective of the SBIR Phase I $250,000 budget is validating sub-40 nanosecond arc-fault quenching on a 480-Volt 3-phase microgrid. Traditional mechanical contactors require 30 to 50 milliseconds to physically separate, which is too slow to prevent electromagnetic interference (EMI) cascading failures in the tactical trailer environment.

Silicon Carbide (SiC) Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field-Effect Transistors (MOSFETs) provide the necessary switching speed and thermal threshold to act as solid-state circuit breakers.

## 2. PROCUREMENT SPECIFICATIONS

* **Required Quantity:** 4 Units (Engineering Samples for HIL Test Bench)
