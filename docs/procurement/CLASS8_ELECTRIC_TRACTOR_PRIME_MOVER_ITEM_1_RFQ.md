# HVF Procurement: Class-8 Electric Tractor Prime Mover (Item 1) RFQ
**Issuing Entity:** Humphrey Virtual Farms LLC (CAGE: 1AHA8 | UEI: S1M4ENLHTDH5)  
**Budget Allocation:** $385,000.00 (CapEx, Contractor-Acquired Property)  
**Target Vendors:** Freightliner (Daimler Truck NA - eCascadia) / Peterbilt (PACCAR - 579EV) / Kenworth (T680E)  

## Technical Requirements
- **Chassis & Class:** Class-8 Heavy Duty Day Cab Tractor (82,000 lbs GVWR allowed under ZEV federal provisions), 6x4 tandem drive axle configuration.
- **Electric Propulsion:** Continuous electric drive rating >= 400 hp (300 kW), peak rating >= 500 hp (375 kW); minimum 23,000 lb-ft wheel torque.
- **Buffer Battery Bank:** 150 kWh to 250 kWh nominal battery capacity (LFP or NMC with thermal liquid conditioning).
- **Mechanical Integration & Frame Envelope:**
  - Clear frame-rail space forward of the drive tandems for integration of dual 150-gal aluminum saddle tanks (49 CFR § 393.67 compliant).
  - Heavy-duty cast sliding fifth wheel rated for >= 55,000 lbs vertical kingpin load.
- **High-Voltage Auxiliary Coupling (Trailer umbilical):**
  - High-voltage DC bus interlock port (750V DC nominal) to accept continuous 100kW–150kW generation from the trailer-mounted micro-turbine while in rolling transit.
  - SAE J1939 CAN bus umbilical link for dynamic traction coordination between tractor e-Drive and trailer powered eAxle.
- **Statutory & Title Protection:**
  - Direct commercial vehicle purchase under Contractor-Acquired Property provisions.
  - 100% commercial title vested exclusively in Humphrey Virtual Farms LLC (10 U.S.C. § 4022).
  - Factory installation of permanent MIL-STD-130N asset title plate (CAGE: 1AHA8).
