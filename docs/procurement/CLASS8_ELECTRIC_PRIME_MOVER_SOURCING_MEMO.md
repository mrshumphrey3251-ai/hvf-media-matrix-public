# COMPONENT SELECTION: CLASS-8 ELECTRIC PRIME MOVER
**Contractor Entity:** Humphrey Virtual Farms LLC (CAGE: 1AHA8)
**System Sub-Assembly:** Project E.B.O.N.Y. Tactical Powertrain Base Platform
**Sourcing Target:** Freightliner eCascadia or Peterbilt 579EV (Commercial Off-The-Shelf)

---

## 1. TECHNICAL JUSTIFICATION FOR COTS MODIFICATION
Project E.B.O.N.Y. requires a 480-Volt / 600-Volt DC high-power traction architecture to integrate the 150kW Series-Hybrid Turbine Auxiliary Power Unit (APU) and trailer electric axle (eAxle). 

To minimize Department of Defense (DoD) developmental risk and acquisition cost, Humphrey Virtual Farms LLC will utilize a Commercial Off-The-Shelf (COTS) Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV) Class-8 tractor as the base platform. Our proprietary engineering scope is splicing the 150kW turbine and the Silicon Carbide (SiC) microgrid protection circuit into this existing DC-link.

## 2. AUTONOMOUS OPERATOR INTEGRATION (DRIVE-BY-WIRE)
Per CEO directive, the E.B.O.N.Y. system will not merely manage the powertrain; it will serve as the autonomous operator of the vehicle for unmanned tactical logistics and leader-follower convoy operations.
* The selected Prime Mover MUST be equipped with a **Drive-by-Wire (DbW)** interface.
* The E.B.O.N.Y. computing core must be able to inject digital Controller Area Network (CAN) commands (J1939 protocol) to control steering, braking, and throttle actuators with zero human intervention.

## 3. PROCUREMENT SPECIFICATIONS
* **Target Platform (Primary):** Freightliner eCascadia (Day Cab, 438 kWh battery, DbW enabled).
* **Target Platform (Alternate):** Peterbilt 579EV (Day Cab, 396 kWh battery, DbW enabled).
* **Payload Capacity Requirement:** 82,000 lbs Gross Combined Weight Rating (GCWR).
* **Modification Constraint:** The selected platform must permit access to the High-Voltage (HV) DC junction box to parallel the 150kW turbine DC/DC converter.
