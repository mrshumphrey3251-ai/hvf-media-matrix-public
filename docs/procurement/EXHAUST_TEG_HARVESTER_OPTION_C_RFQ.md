## Technical Requirements
- 5.0kW - 8.0kW continuous DC waste-heat recovery from 450°C-550°C turbine exhaust
- Low backpressure annular sleeve (<= 1.5 kPa drop on 6-8" exhaust duct)
- High-temperature Seebeck solid-state cassettes with closed-loop glycol cold-side
- Integrated SiC MPPT boost converter outputting directly to 350V-750V DC bus
- J1939 CAN / Modbus telemetry for delta-T and power monitoring
