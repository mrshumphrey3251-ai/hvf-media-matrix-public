## 1. TECHNICAL JUSTIFICATION FOR EXTENDED RANGE FUEL STORAGE
To achieve the 3,500+ mile unrefueled expeditionary range, the 150kW microturbine requires a total capacity of 950 gallons of JP-8 / Diesel. The Class-8 prime mover supports 300 gallons via frame-mounted saddle tanks. The remaining 650 gallons must be stored within the 53-foot autonomous trailer to ensure compliance with federal axle-weight distribution laws (bridge formulas).

To maximize internal cargo volume for military logistics, the 650-gallon fuel cell will be mounted externally in the "underbelly" of the trailer (between the landing gear and the rear tandem axles). An autonomous transfer system is required to continuously pump fuel from the trailer forward to the prime mover's saddle tanks during transit.

## 2. PROCUREMENT SPECIFICATIONS
* **Target Component:** 650-Gallon Rectangular Aluminum Underbelly Fuel Cell.
* **Material & Baffling:** Heavy-gauge marine aluminum with internal surge baffles to prevent liquid slosh during emergency braking or evasive maneuvers.
* **Compliance Standard:** Must be certified to 49 CFR 393.67 for commercial liquid fuel systems.
* **Transfer Mechanism:** Explosion-proof 24V DC rotary vane transfer pump (rated for continuous duty), integrated with a programmable logic controller (PLC) to automatically activate when the prime mover saddle tanks drop below 40% capacity.
* **Trailer Interconnect:** High-pressure braided steel breakaway lines utilizing dry-break quick disconnects at the tractor-trailer umbilical interface.
