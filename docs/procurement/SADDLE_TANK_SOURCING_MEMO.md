## 1. TECHNICAL JUSTIFICATION FOR PRIME MOVER FUEL STORAGE
To achieve the 3,500+ mile expeditionary range, the 150kW Series-Hybrid Microturbine requires a massive fuel reserve. The total system architecture calls for 950 gallons of JP-8 / Diesel. To maintain vehicle dynamics, weight distribution, and immediate fuel feed to the turbine, 300 gallons of this reserve must be stored directly on the Class-8 prime mover.

Due to the extreme volatility of military heavy fuels, commercial highway testing mandates strict adherence to Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) crash-survivability regulations. 

## 2. PROCUREMENT SPECIFICATIONS
* **Target Component:** Dual 150-Gallon Cylindrical Aluminum Saddle Tanks (300 Gallons Total).
* **Material:** Heavy-gauge non-sparking marine-grade aluminum.
* **Compliance Standard:** Must be certified to 49 CFR 393.67 (Liquid fuel systems for commercial motor vehicles).
* **Mounting Protocol:** Heavy-duty steel J-brackets with rubberized isolation straps for direct frame-rail mounting.
* **Plumbing:** Must include anti-siphon valves, dual-draw/dual-return ports, and crossover equalization lines to maintain weight balance between the driver and passenger sides during operation.
