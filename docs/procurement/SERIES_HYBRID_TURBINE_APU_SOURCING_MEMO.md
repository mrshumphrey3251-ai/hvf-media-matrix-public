## 1. TECHNICAL JUSTIFICATION FOR MICROTURBINE INTEGRATION
The operational mandate for Project E.B.O.N.Y. requires a 3,500+ mile unrefueled expeditionary range in austere environments lacking electrical grid infrastructure. The Class-8 Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV) prime mover acts as the baseline traction platform, but requires continuous, high-efficiency recharging en route.

To achieve this, the architecture employs a Series-Hybrid configuration utilizing a heavy-fuel microturbine. Unlike internal combustion engines, microturbines utilize air-bearing technology (zero oil, zero coolant) and can natively combust military single-fuel types (JP-8, F-24, Diesel) with extreme thermal efficiency. The microturbine operates at a constant RPM to continuously inject DC power into the BEV's high-voltage battery buffer, eliminating range anxiety and grid dependency.

## 2. PROCUREMENT SPECIFICATIONS
* **Power Output Target:** 150 kW Continuous Base Load.
* **Fuel Compatibility:** Multi-fuel capable (JP-8, F-24, standard Diesel, Bio-mass synfuels).
* **Cooling Requirement:** 100% Air-cooled (no liquid radiators or coolant loops).
* **Mechanical Footprint:** Must conform to standard Class-8 chassis frame rails (behind the cab) or integrate into the forward bulkhead of the 53ft autonomous trailer.
* **Output Interface:** Must include an integrated rectifier to output 480V/600V DC directly to the prime mover's high-voltage junction box.
