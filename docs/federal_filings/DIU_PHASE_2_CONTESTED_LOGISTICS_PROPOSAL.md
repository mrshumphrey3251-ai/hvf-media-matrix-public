## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement
In contested Indo-Pacific and arctic operational theaters, bulk fuel supply lines and centralized charging depots are prime targets for precision interdiction. Pure battery-electric logistics tractors lose 8,000 lbs of cargo payload to battery weight and require 4 to 8 years of utility grid upgrades. 

Project E.B.O.N.Y. Phase 2 delivers a Class-8 series-hybrid freight tractor and smart-trailer ecosystem capable of 7,500 continuous highway miles without depot charging or en-route commercial refueling.

---

## 2. Technical Architecture & Demonstration Deliverables
1. **Series-Hybrid Class-8 Tractor:**
   - 400 hp continuous electric drive with dual 150-gallon frame-rail aluminum saddle tanks.
   - High-voltage DC umbilical linking tractor traction motors to trailer energy generation.
2. **Tactical Power Trailer:**
   - 650-gallon underbelly JP-8/diesel fuel cell (950 gal total system fuel reserve).
   - 150 kW multi-fuel micro-turbine APU operating as a continuous high-efficiency range extender.
   - 500 kWh NDAA-compliant KORE Power LFP battery bank buffering heavy acceleration surges.
   - Powered trailer eAxle providing auxiliary traction and regenerative braking on highway grades.
   - 18 kW anti-glint matte ETFE tactical solar canopy and exhaust TEG waste-heat recovery.
3. **Expeditionary Islanded Microgrid:**
   - Victron 480V 3-phase pure sine-wave inverter export feeder (150 kVA continuous / 300 kVA surge).
   - Autonomous field support for rapid airfield damage recovery (RADR) concrete batch plants (60 yd³/hr).
4. **Sovereign Command & Hardware Hardening:**
   - MIL-STD-188-125 EMP enclosure with Crystal Group rugged compute server.
   - 36.75ns Silicon Carbide (SiC) analog kinetic interlock for sub-microsecond arc-flash and fault clearing.
   - Optical hardware data diode enforcing unidirectional SCADA telemetry egress (NIST SP 800-82 Rev 2).
