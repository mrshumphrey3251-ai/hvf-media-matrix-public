## 1. COMMERCIAL MARKET SIGNIFICANCE & DEMAND DRIVERS
* **Core Market Pain Point:** Current Class-8 Battery-Electric Vehicles (BEVs) are restricted to short-haul drayage (sub-300 mile operational radius) due to the absence of nationwide megawatt charging corridors and severe grid capacity constraints.
* **Dual-Use Value Proposition:** 
  * **Commercial Sector:** Enables long-haul fleet carriers to operate extended routes (3,500 to 4,200 miles) without reliance on public high-power charging infrastructure, using lower-emission multi-fuel turbines and regenerative trailer electric axles (eAxles).
  * **Defense / Austere Logistics:** Solves contested logistics constraints by providing self-deploying tactical command hubs and 480-Volt 3-phase microgrid export capacity in operational theaters where refueling infrastructure is degraded or non-existent.

---

## 2. PHASE II TRANSITION & PROTOTYPING ROADMAP
* **Phase I Milestone Gateway (Months 1–6):** De-risk the powertrain controls and high-voltage circuit protection using Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) benchtop instrumentation ($250,000 statutory cap).
* **Phase II Objective (Months 7–24):** Secure a $1.2M to $1.8M Phase II federal award to fabricate the first operational demonstrator vehicle:
  * Procure and integrate the Class-8 glider chassis, 53-ft trailer lab subframe, and 150 kW turbine Auxiliary Power Unit (APU).
  * Deploy the validated Command and Control (C2) software stack into the physical automotive environment.
* **Follow-On Phase III / Commercial Funding:** Leverage private fleet testing agreements and federal commercialization programs (e.g., Defense Innovation Unit Commercial Solutions Openings) to scale manufacturing without equity dilution.
