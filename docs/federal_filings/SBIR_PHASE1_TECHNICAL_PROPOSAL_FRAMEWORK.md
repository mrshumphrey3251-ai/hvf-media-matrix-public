## 1. IDENTIFICATION AND SIGNIFICANCE OF THE INNOVATION
* **Problem Statement:** Current commercial Class-8 electric vehicle (EV) combinations are tethered to static megawatt charging stations, restricting operational radius to sub-300 miles and rendering them unusable in austere, degraded, or zero-infrastructure environments.
* **Technical Innovation:** A software-controlled series-hybrid architecture combining high-voltage SiC solid-state fault interlocks (<40ns isolation), trailer-distributed eAxle regeneration, and an auxiliary micro-turbine generator to achieve an unrefueled heavy-haul range of 3,500–4,200 miles within 80,000 lb Federal Bridge Formula limits (49 CFR 658.17).

## 2. TECHNICAL OBJECTIVES (PHASE I - 6 MONTHS)
* **Objective 1 (Software-in-the-Loop):** Validate real-time telemetry streaming and Merkle DAG single-byte tamper detection across simulated CAN bus nodes under 100% bus saturation.
* **Objective 2 (Hardware-in-the-Loop Microgrid):** Build a 480V 3-phase benchtop power electronics test bench integrating a 1200V SiC gate driver to confirm sub-microsecond arc-fault quenching.
* **Objective 3 (Dyno-Coupled Energy Balance):** Model real-world rolling resistance, wind shear, and regenerative capture profiles to confirm a minimum 3,500-mile range envelope using 950 gallons of onboard diesel/JP-8 buffer.

## 3. WORK PLAN & DELIVERABLES (WORK BREAKDOWN STRUCTURE)

## 4. RELATED R&D AND PRIOR WORK (TRL 4 BASELINE)

## 5. COMMERCIALIZATION & TRANSITION STRATEGY
* **Phase II Target:** Physical integration of a 53ft mobile command and battery trailer with commercial glider tractor (Target budget: $1.2M - $1.8M non-dilutive federal match).
* **Commercial Applications:** Remote disaster response microgrids, isolated agricultural energy hubs, and long-haul intermodal logistics.
