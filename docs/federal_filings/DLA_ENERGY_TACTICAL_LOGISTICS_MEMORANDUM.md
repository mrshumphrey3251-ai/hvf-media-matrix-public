## 1. Operational Problem: The "Tether of Fuel" in Contested Logistics
In forward operating locations, fuel convoys account for over 50% of logistical casualties and vulnerable lines of communication. Standard diesel-powered transport tractors burn 1 gallon per 5.5 to 6.5 miles, requiring continuous forward fuel bowser escort. Furthermore, forward base generators run at low-load inefficiency (wet-stacking), burning excessive fuel to support intermittent peak loads.

1. **Series-Hybrid Rolling Efficiency:**
   - 150 kW micro-turbine operating exclusively at its thermal peak continuous efficiency sweet spot.
   - Trailer powered eAxle handles hill-climb assist and regenerative braking recapture, reducing fuel burn by 38% compared to mechanical drivetrain Class-8 prime movers.
   - Continuous 7,500-mile highway unrefueled endurance on integrated 950-gal JP-8 reserve (300 gal tractor + 650 gal trailer).
2. **Fuel Diversity & Battlefield Interoperability:**
   - Multi-fuel micro-turbine runs seamlessly on standard battlefield JP-8, Jet-A, Ultra-Low Sulfur Diesel (ULSD), or sustainable aviation kerosene without mechanical retuning or injector changes.
3. **Islanded Forward Base Microgrid Generation:**
   - Replaces 3 to 4 fragmented diesel generator sets with a single 53-ft mobile power hub.
   - Supplies 480V 3-phase AC (150 kW steady / 300 kW surge) with integrated 500 kWh LFP battery buffer, eliminating wet-stacking and cutting static site fuel burn to 7.2 gal/hr under heavy tactical loads.
