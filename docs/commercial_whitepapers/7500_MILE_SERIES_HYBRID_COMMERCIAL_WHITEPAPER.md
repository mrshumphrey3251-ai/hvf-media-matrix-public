# Commercial Whitepaper: 7,500-Mile Series-Hybrid Fleet Architecture
**Entity:** Humphrey Virtual Farms LLC (CAGE: 1AHA8 | UEI: S1M4ENLHTDH5)  
**Author:** Jeffery Humphrey, Founder & CEO  
**Target:** Tier-1 Commercial Carriers (Walmart, FedEx Freight, UPS)  

## Executive Summary
- **The Problem:** CARB ACF and EPA Phase 3 mandates collide with utility grid delays (4-8 years) and BEV payload loss (-8,000 lbs).
- **The Solution:** Series-hybrid Class-8 tractor + 53-ft trailer subframe with 950-gal fuel reservoir, 150kW micro-turbine, powered eAxle, and exhaust TEG.
- **Performance:** 7,500 continuous highway miles without en-route charging or refueling; $0 depot electrical upgrade required.
- **Economics:** 5-year net savings of $34.5M across 500 tractors versus pure battery-electric fleets.
