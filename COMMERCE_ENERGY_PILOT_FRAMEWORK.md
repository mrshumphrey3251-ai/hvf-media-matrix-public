### Phase 1: Software-Defined Baseline Ingestion (Days 1–30)
* **Zero-CAPEX Integration:** No physical hardware installation required. 
* Ingest historical facility utility data (Green Button standard), existing smart meter feeds, or facility SCADA historian exports directly into the C2 enclave.
* Establish the baseline power utilization and identify historical peak demand tariff penalties.

### Phase 2: Algorithmic Load Profiling (Days 31–60)
* Autonomous identification of high-draw machinery sequences driving peak billing events using the ingested historical data.
* Generation of a deterministic simulation proving exact cost-reduction ROI if Ebony had been actively managing the load.
* Finalization of the hardware footprint required for active control (to be funded by utility partner or state energy grant).

### Phase 3: Active Autonomous Peak Shaving (Days 61–120)
* Integration with facility-provided or grant-funded edge IoT controllers.
* Activation of deterministic load-switching logic to mitigate demand spikes.
* Autonomous dispatch of local battery storage and renewable assets during peak tariff windows.

### Phase 4: Full WORM Ledger Integration & Certification (Day 121+)
* Cryptographic sealing of all kilowatt-hour generation and consumption events into append-only storage.
* Issuance of verifiable audit certificates for utility interconnection compliance and clean energy incentives.
