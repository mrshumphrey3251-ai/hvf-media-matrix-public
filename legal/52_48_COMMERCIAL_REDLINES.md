\## 52/48 COMMERCIAL BREAKDOWN - WITH HVF REDLINE AMENDMENTS



\*\*Executive Summary\*\*

The proposed 52/48 structure is designed for the jointly created integrated venture or commercial ecosystem.

It preserves each party's pre-existing company and core intellectual property while establishing a clear default allocation for designated joint ownership and joint commercial proceeds.

> \*\*\[HVF REDLINE AMENDMENT]:\*\* Governance and Voting Rights are locked at 52% HVF / 48% SignalLink. Economic Distributions are decoupled from Governance and are locked at an equal 50/50 split of the Founder Distribution Pool, as defined in Section 3.



\*\*1. What the 52/48 Split Covers\*\*

\* Equity or economic participation in a newly formed joint venture, if one is created.

\* Designated jointly developed products, integrations, and commercial assets.

\* Net distributable proceeds from customer opportunities sold as the joint Project Ebony x SignalLink offering.

> \*\*\[HVF REDLINE AMENDMENT]:\*\* "Net distributable proceeds" are superseded by the Humphrey Treasury Matrix defined in Section 3.



\*\*2. What the 52/48 Split Does NOT Cover\*\*

\* It does not give HVF 48% of SignalLink Protocol LLC.

\* It does not give SignalLink 52% of Humphrey Virtual Farm.

\* It does not automatically transfer either party's pre-existing patents, copyrights, source code, trade secrets, customer lists, brands, domains, data, credentials, or other background assets.

\* It does not automatically apply to business either party conducts independently without using the joint offering.

> \*\*\[HVF REDLINE AMENDMENT]:\*\* Mutual Exclusivity and Clean Hands. Both parties agree to a strict non-compete within the defined domain of this Joint Venture. Any pre-existing deals must be disclosed in a formal schedule prior to signing. Undisclosed deals are nullified from exemption.



\*\*3. Revenue Waterfall - Default Proposal\*\*

> \*\*\[HVF REDLINE AMENDMENT]:\*\* Section 3 is formally stricken in its entirety and replaced with the Humphrey Treasury Matrix.

> 

> \*\*Gross Revenue Allocation:\*\* Upon receipt of client funds (post-third-party processing fees and approved COGS), all revenue immediately flows into a rigid four-tier treasury system:

> 1. 30% - Tax Escrow: Held exclusively for federal/state/corporate tax liabilities.

> 2. 15% - Operating Expenses (OpEx): Allocated for dual-signature approved daily operations.

> 3. 15% - Ecosystem CapEx: Locked exclusively for future technological build-outs and R\&D.

> 4. 40% - Founder Distributions: The net profit pool.

> 

> \*\*Economic Split:\*\* The 40% Founder Distribution pool is split equally (50/50), resulting in a net 20% total revenue payout to HVF and a 20% total revenue payout to SignalLink.

> \*\*Distribution SLA:\*\* Founder Distributions must be calculated and wired within Net-15 days of client funds clearing.



\*\*4. Cost Controls\*\*

\* Costs must be documented and attributable to the joint project.

\* Material expenses should be approved before commitment.

> \*\*\[HVF REDLINE AMENDMENT]:\*\* "Material" is stricken. ALL expenses, travel, and subcontractor costs must be pre-approved via Dual-Signature Authorization (Jeffery Humphrey \& Drew Phillips Jr.) prior to commitment. Unapproved expenses are the sole responsibility of the incurring party.

\* No unilateral founder salary, management fee, affiliate markup, licensing fee, or overhead allocation should reduce the other party's share unless agreed in writing.

\* Accounting records supporting the revenue waterfall should be available to both parties on reasonable notice.

> \*\*\[HVF REDLINE AMENDMENT]:\*\* "Reasonable notice" is stricken. HVF retains absolute Open-Book Audit Rights. SignalLink must produce all accounting records and receipts within 48 hours of a written request.



\*\*5. Customer-Originated Opportunities\*\*

\* If one party independently brings an opportunity but sells the full joint offering, the default 52/48 economics still apply unless the parties sign a transaction-specific exception.

\* If the opportunity uses only one party's independent product or service and does not use the joint offering, the revenue belongs to that party.

\* If a transaction includes both independent and joint components, the statement of work or deal sheet should allocate revenue and costs by component before applying the joint split.

> \*\*\[HVF REDLINE AMENDMENT]:\*\* Pro-Rata Allocation Authority. The allocation of revenue between independent and joint components cannot be determined unilaterally. It must be explicitly defined and subject to Dual-Signature Authorization prior to signing the client contract. If no agreement is reached, the joint component is stripped, and the originating party retains 100%.
