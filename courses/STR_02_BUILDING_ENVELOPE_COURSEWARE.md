## MODULE 1: ASTM C1153 RADIOMETRIC THERMAL ROOF MOISTURE SURVEYING
### 1.1 Thermodynamics of Moisture Entrapment
Commercial low-slope roof membranes (EPDM, TPO, PVC, Modified Bitumen) store solar radiation during daylight hours.
* **Thermal Capacitance Principle:** Water possesses a specific heat capacity (4.184 J/g*K) approximately four times greater than dry roofing insulation. When a roof membrane is breached, the underlying insulation acts as a sponge.
* **Nocturnal Radiant Cooling:** To detect this trapped moisture, inspections cannot be flown during the day. Flight missions must initiate **45 to 90 minutes post-sunset** during dry surface conditions. The dry roof substrate rapidly radiates its heat to the clear sky, cooling significantly. The saturated, wet insulation retains its heat and continues emitting infrared radiation.

### 1.2 Radiometric Emissivity & Thermal Contrast
* **Emissivity Calibration:** Inspectors must configure the radiometric thermal cores (such as the FLIR LWIR) to match the surface material. For single-ply membranes (EPDM, TPO), the emissivity factor (epsilon) must be set to **0.92**.
* **Anomaly Detection:** A thermal contrast anomaly of **Delta T >= 2.0 degrees C** relative to the dry background indicates active subsurface insulation saturation.
* **Remediation Cost Modeling:** If the moisture saturation covers < 25% of the gross roof area, recommend a localized overlay repair. If saturation is >= 25%, the entire roof requires a full tear-off and replacement.

## MODULE 2: TITLE 61 BUILDING ENVELOPE AIR LOSS AUDITING
### 2.1 Thermal Bridging & Curtain Wall Air Infiltration
Inspectors quantify perimeter thermal energy loss across state-owned commercial facilities under the Oklahoma State Energy Plan (SEP):
* **Insulated Glazing Failure:** Detect seal degradation in double-pane commercial curtain walls; failed argon gas fill produces distinct center-of-glass thermal anomalies.
* **Flight Standoff Safety:** Maintain a strict **10-foot minimum standoff distance** from the parapet edge during autonomous mapping to mitigate turbulent vortex shedding near the roofline.

### 2.2 Conductive BTU Heat Loss Formulation
Energy loss is calculated using the standard heat transfer equation:
Q = U * A * Delta_T
*(Where Q is the total heat transfer, U is the overall heat transfer coefficient, A is the anomaly surface area, and Delta_T is the indoor-to-outdoor temperature differential).*

## MODULE 3: HIGH-RISE MASONRY FACADE SPALLING
### 3.1 Rust-Jacking & Concrete Spall Detection
* **Structural Steel Corrosion:** Moisture penetration behind historic masonry causes concealed structural shelf angles and steel lintels to oxidize. 
* **Rust-Jacking Mechanics:** Oxidizing steel expands up to 10 times its original volume, generating internal tensile stresses exceeding **10,000 psi**. This massive force physically cracks and pushes the exterior masonry facade off the building, a process known as "rust-jacking."
* **Photogrammetry Stand-Off:** Deploy 48MP zoom sensors at a **30-meter standoff distance** to measure vertical shear cracks down to **0.2 mm** in width.
