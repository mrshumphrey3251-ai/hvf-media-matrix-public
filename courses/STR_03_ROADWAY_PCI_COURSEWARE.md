## MODULE 1: ASTM D6433 PAVEMENT CONDITION INDEX (PCI)
### 1.1 Asphalt Distress Classification
Roadway evaluation requires inspecting, identifying, and measuring 19 distinct pavement distress types. The primary defects are:
* **Fatigue (Alligator) Cracking:** Interconnected cracks resembling chicken wire or alligator hide, caused by repeated traffic wheel loadings over a weakened subgrade. Severity ranges from Low (fine hairline cracks) to High (pieces loose or rocking under traffic).
* **Block Cracking:** Rectangular blocks caused by asphalt binder volume shrinkage and age hardening, not traffic load.
* **Transverse & Longitudinal Joint Cracking:** Cracks parallel or perpendicular to the roadway centerline caused by paving joint settlement.
* **Potholes:** Bowl-shaped depressions in the pavement surface >2 inches in diameter.

### 1.2 Mathematical PCI Deduct Formulation
Inspectors calculate the Pavement Condition Index (PCI) on a 0 to 100 numerical scale:
PCI = 100 - SUM(CDV)
*(Where CDV is the Corrected Deduct Value determined by plotting distress density against ASTM D6433 standard curves).*

**Rating Scale & Municipal Action:**
* **85-100 (Good):** Routine maintenance only.
* **70-84 (Satisfactory):** Prioritize thin-lift asphalt preservation or preventative seal coat.
* **55-69 (Fair):** Preventative overlay required.
* **0-54 (Poor / Failed):** Structural failure of base layers; full structural reconstruction mandated.

## MODULE 2: MOBILE LIDAR PROFILOMETRY & WHEEL-PATH RUTTING
### 2.1 Transverse Cross-Slope & Rut Depth Extraction
* **LiDAR Acquisition:** High-speed mobile laser scanning at **300,000 points/sec** generates a millimeter-accurate digital elevation model (DEM) point cloud of the pavement surface.
* **Virtual Straightedge Profiling:** Algorithmically lay a virtual 6-foot transverse straightedge across the vehicle wheel paths to extract the maximum depression depth:
d_rut = y_straightedge - y_pavement
* **Hydroplaning Threshold:** A rut depth of **d_rut >= 0.25 inches** holds standing water and creates a severe hydroplaning hazard, triggering an immediate safety overlay mandate under ODOT standards.

## MODULE 3: DRAINAGE CULVERTS & FLIGHT PROTOCOLS
### 3.1 Reinforced Concrete Box (RCB) Culvert Inspections
* **Barrel Structural Integrity:** Inspect interior barrel joints for separation. Joint separation **>1.0 inch** indicates severe foundation undermining and soil migration.
* **Hydrologic Capacity:** Measure silt accumulation obstructing the design hydrologic flow using the Rational Runoff Equation: Q = C * I * A.

### 3.2 FAA Part 107 Operations Over Moving Traffic
When executing low-altitude right-of-way (ROW) mapping near active logistics thoroughfares:
* The Remote Pilot in Command (RPIC) must maintain continuous visual line of sight, utilizing an active visual observer in a moving roadway escort vehicle.
* **Traffic Law:** Part 107 Category 3 and 4 UAS compliance prohibits sustained flight directly above moving vehicles without closed-lane traffic control.
