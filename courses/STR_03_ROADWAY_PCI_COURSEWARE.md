# STR-03: Roadway Pavement Distress, Rutting & ASTM D6433 PCI Survey
**Accredited Training Courseware // Sovereign Field Academy**
**Course Duration:** 40 Hours | **Framework:** ODOT & ASTM D6433 Standards

## UNIT 1: ASTM D6433 PAVEMENT CONDITION INDEX (PCI)
* **Categories:** Alligator cracking, block cracking, potholes, raveling.
* **PCI Formulation:** PCI = 100 - SUM(CDV). Scores range from 100 (Good) to 0 (Failed).

## UNIT 2: MOBILE LIDAR PROFILOMETRY & RIGHT-OF-WAY CULVERTS
* **Rutting Profiles:** High-speed RTK LiDAR sweeps pavement. Rut depth >= 0.25 inches triggers safety overlay.
* **Drainage Triage:** Inspect RCB culverts for settlement and headwall siltation.
