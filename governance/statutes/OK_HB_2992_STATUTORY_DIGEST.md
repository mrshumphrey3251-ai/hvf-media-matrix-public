# STATUTORY DIGEST: OKLAHOMA HOUSE BILL 2992
**Short Title:** Public Building Construction and Infrastructure Energy Efficiency Act  
**Codified In:** 61 O.S. § 212 et seq.  
**State Oversight Agency:** Oklahoma Department of Commerce / Office of Management and Enterprise Services (OMES)  

---

### 1. LEGISLATIVE PURPOSE
HB 2992 establishes mandatory protocols for improving energy efficiency, power operational resilience, and automated utility management across critical public facilities, municipal infrastructure, and state-backed industrial projects in Oklahoma.

### 2. CORE STATUTORY PROVISIONS
1. **Mandatory Load Benchmarking:**
   - Requires continuous monitoring of electrical demand (kW) and energy consumption (kWh).
   - Directs the implementation of automated supervisory control systems to identify and eliminate high-cost peak-demand spikes.

2. **Distributed & Resilient Power Integration:**
   - Authorizes and encourages integration of behind-the-meter (BTM) power resources (solar, battery energy storage, micro-generation).
   - Requires infrastructure resilience against sudden grid curtailment, localized brownouts, and transmission failures.

3. **Life-Cycle Operational Cost Accountability:**
   - Mandates that state-funded infrastructure investments account for the long-term operational cost of downtime, equipment damage from dirty power, and utility tariffs.
