# Executive Engineering Directive - ISO-9001-RACK-FRM (RACK-FRM-001)
## Modular Steel Frame for Crystal Group RS-Series Server Rack Mounting
**Prepared by:** E.B.O.N.Y. - Project Management Interface  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-11 | **Status:** APPROVED (MFA Token: 515757)

### 1. Scope & Load Summary
- Total Supported Mass: 1,000 kg (Crystal Group RS-Series Rack: 800 kg, Ancillary PDUs/Cabling: 200 kg)
- Static Load (SL): 9.81 kN
- Dynamic Shock Load (DL, 3g rough-terrain envelope): 29.43 kN
- Design Load (FoS = 1.5): 44.15 kN (Rated for >= 44 kN in any vector)

### 2. Structural Steel Alloy & Frame Extrusions
- Primary Uprights: 50x50x10mm H-section, ASTM A572 Grade 50 (Yield: 345 MPa, Tensile: 450 MPa)
- Floor/Ceiling Cross-Members: 100x50x8mm C-section, ASTM A572 Grade 50
- Diagonal Bracing: 40x40x8mm square tube, ASTM A572 Grade 50
- Isolator Anchor Plates: 300x300x12mm, ASTM A514 Grade C (Yield: 415 MPa)

### 3. Vibration & Shock Attenuation (MIL-STD-810H Method 514.8)
- Configuration per Upright (4 uprights total):
  - 2x Dual-layer polyurethane elastomeric pads (Vibra-Flex EP-200) -> 24 kN capacity, 0.25 damping ratio
  - 2x Visco-elastic stainless wire-rope isolators (Rope-Damp WR-150) -> 30 kN capacity, 0.30 damping ratio
- Total Isolation Capacity per Upright: 54 kN (exceeds 44.15 kN design load with FoS >= 1.2 on isolation system)

### 4. Tie-In Hardpoints & Fasteners
- Floor & Ceiling Anchor Plates: M24 Grade 8 (ASTM A325), 140 kN tensile rating, torqued to 180 Nm with Loctite 641
- Side Wall Stabilizers: M20 Grade 8, 95 kN shear rating, torqued to 150 Nm
- Ultrasonic NDT inspection across >= 90% of structural weld length
