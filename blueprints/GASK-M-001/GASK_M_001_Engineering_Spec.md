# Executive Engineering Directive - GASK-M-001
## MIL-SPEC Fire-Rated, CBRN-Resistant & EMI/EMP-Shielded Gaskets (FIRE-GASK-M)
**Prepared by:** E.B.O.N.Y. - Project Management Interface  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-11 | **Status:** APPROVED (MFA Token: 515757)

### 1. Scope & Multi-Threat Barrier Architecture
- Item Target: p2-5 - Seal all chassis, floor, wall, and roof penetrations
- Multi-Layer Composite:
  1. Interior Layer: 3.0mm two-component silicone intumescent sealant (ASTM E814 F-Rating >= 3, UL 1479 T-Rating <= 0.35 W/m*K)
  2. Shielding Core: 0.5mm encapsulated Beryllium-Copper (BeCu) woven mesh (MIL-DTL-83528, >= 80 dB attenuation across 30 kHz - 1 GHz)
  3. Exterior Barrier: 0.2mm perfluorinated polymer (PFPE) CBRN barrier (MIL-STD-810H Method 504.3 permeation <= 0.1 ug*cm^-2*min^-1)

### 2. Compression & Mechanical Installation
- Standard Gasket Form Factor: 3.7mm total nominal thickness with silicone adhesive backing (30 psi shear)
- Compression Envelope: 20% +/- 2% original thickness using M8-M12 fasteners torqued to 4.5 Nm +/- 0.3 Nm
- Seam Overlap: >= 10mm overlap sealed with conductive silicone seam filler

### 3. Positive-Pressure Hull & Quality Verification
- Positive-Pressure Leak Test: Pressurize interior to +150 Pa (+/- 5 Pa) for 30 min per MIL-STD-202; leakage rate <= 0.5 L/min
- Mechanical Durability: 2-hour random vibration (10-2000 Hz, 5g RMS) + 20g shock per MIL-STD-810H Method 501/516
- Safety & Traceability: NIOSH 42 CFR 84 PPE protocol for BeCu; lot serialized to MCC-GASK-TEST-DB
