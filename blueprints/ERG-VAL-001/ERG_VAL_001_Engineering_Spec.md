### 1. Scope & Human-Factors Compliance
- Governing Standards: ISO 9241-210, MIL-STD-1472H, U.S. Army ANSUR II / NATO STANAG 3350, NFPA 101
- Anthropometric Population: 5th-to-95th percentile operator stature (`1580 mm – 1970 mm`) and sitting eye height (`1060 mm – 1240 mm`)
- Certification Role: Final Phase-2 Integration validation milestone (`PH2-ERG-VAL-2026-024`)

### 2. Dual-Redundant Control Reach Envelopes
- Hall-Effect Controls: Symmetrical left/right positioning (`300 mm` forward, `+/- 150 mm` lateral) within `650 mm – 950 mm` forward and `500 mm – 850 mm` lateral reach envelopes (`<= 30°` shoulder abduction)
- Center-Front E-Stop: Positioned `250 mm` forward at centerline (`850 mm` height) for bilateral stress actuation (`<= 2 N` trip force)

### 3. 7-Display Command Wall Viewing Geometry & Luminance
- Viewing Distance: `800 mm` nominal seated eye-to-screen baseline
- Center Theater (55-in 4K UHD): `30° +/- 2°` horizontal FOV, `17° +/- 2°` vertical FOV, `300 – 500 cd/m²` luminance, `>= 1500:1` contrast ratio, `< 10%` surface glare
- Flanking Touchscreens (6x 27-in QHD): `12° +/- 1°` horizontal FOV each, `250 – 400 cd/m²` luminance, `>= 1200:1` contrast ratio, `>= 9 mm` interactive touch target sizing

### 4. Cabin Acoustics, Emergency Egress & Phase-2 Merkle Closure
- Acoustic Ceilings: Continuous ambient noise `<= 55 dBA` at operator ear under full ECU/inverter load; peak impulsive transient `<= 70 dBA` (`0.5 s` max)
- Emergency Egress: `>= 900 mm` unobstructed exit aisle, `850 mm` door swing clearance, `>= 10 lux` emergency lighting, and `<= 15 s` seated-to-exterior egress timing
- Phase-2 Ledger Closure: Anchored under ledger entry `PH2-ERG-VAL-2026-024` (`PHASE_2_INTEGRATION_COMPLETE`)
