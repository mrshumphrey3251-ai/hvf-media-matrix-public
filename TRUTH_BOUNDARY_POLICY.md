## 1. Executive Directive: Zero-Simulation Standard
In accordance with standing executive command, Project Ebony software, artificial intelligence agents, and operational interfaces are strictly forbidden from generating, reporting, or calculating synthetic data, mock scoring algorithms, or simulated states unless explicitly requested by CEO Humphrey with an active `--allow-simulation` flag.

## 2. Four Pillars of Data Provenance
All system metrics, reports, voice alerts, and repository commits must belong to one of three verified provenance classes:
1. **`PHYSICAL_HARDWARE`**: Raw sensor feeds, physical Modbus coil state confirmations, hardware watchdog pings, and serial transceiver telemetry.
2. **`CRYPTOGRAPHIC_DATABASE`**: Cryptographically signed SQLite records (`ebony_active_state.db`, `hvf_memory_vault.db`) backed by Ed25519-signed Merkle ledger blocks.
3. **`GOVERNMENT_PORTAL_VERIFIED`**: Authoritative data originating directly from official federal government procurement portals (e.g., Tradewinds Submission `9-26-3703` verified as `Compliant/Queued for Assessment`).

Any data outside these three pillars is classified as **`SIMULATION_PROHIBITED`** and will be rejected at the architectural level.

## 3. Tradewinds & Federal Procurement Guardrails
- **No Client-Side Scoring:** No internal code or script may calculate an "award probability" or claim award status.
- **Authoritative Portal Truth:** Official Tradewinds status is determined exclusively by formal notifications from CDAO and ACC-RI through the official portal.
- **Marketplace Distinctions:** The system strictly maintains the distinction between "Awardable Status" (listing on the marketplace) and a "Funded Contract Award" (obligated federal program dollars).
