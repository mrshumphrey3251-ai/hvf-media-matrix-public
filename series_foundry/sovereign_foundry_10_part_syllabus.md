### Executive Overview
Humphrey Virtual Farms LLC announces "The Sovereign Foundry," a 10-part technical and industrial series outlining the physical production, environmental hardening, and federal transition of sovereign, sub-30W bare-metal defense hardware.

### Series Structure
- **Phase I: The Physical Foundry (Parts 01–05):** Focuses on rural defense manufacturing (Hinton, OK), domestic silicon supply chain independence, MIL-STD qualification, and low-power edge unit economics.
- **Phase II: Defense Capitalization & Production Scaling (Parts 06–10):** Details federal prototype-to-production transitions (10 U.S.C. § 4022/4023), multi-agency acquisition partitioning, defense prime integration, and dual-use commercial utility deployment.
